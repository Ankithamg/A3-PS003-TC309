ALGORITHM: Federated Averaging for Traffic Forecasting

Input:
  K = 5 virtual clients
  E = local epochs
  T = communication rounds
  public time-series traffic data
  seeds = {0, 1, 2}

Preprocess:
  1. Split time chronologically into 60% train, 20% validation, 20% test.
  2. Fit normalization statistics on train timestamps only.
  3. Create windows: previous 12 observations -> next observation.
  4. Partition train windows:
       IID: randomly distribute windows across K clients.
       Non-IID: assign disjoint groups of sensors to clients.

For each seed:
  Train centralized GRU on pooled training windows.
  Evaluate centralized model on test windows.

  For partition in {IID, Non-IID}:
    Initialize global GRU weights w.
    For round t = 1..T:
      For each client k:
        Send w to client k.
        Train local model for E epochs on D_k.
        Receive local weights w_k and sample count n_k.
      Update global weights:
        w = sum_k (n_k / sum_j(n_j)) * w_k
      Count communication:
        bytes_t = 2 * K * parameter_count * bytes_per_parameter
      Record validation MAE and cumulative bytes.
    Evaluate final global model on test windows.
    Record MAE, RMSE, communication and convergence.

Aggregate metrics over seeds 0, 1, 2.
